Paste, pull, keep.

  1. Paste a place

    A Google Maps link of any kind, a street address, a landmark, or raw coordinates like 40.7411, -73.9897.

  2. We pull the block

    Every footprint, height, road, park, river and tree inside your radius comes from OpenStreetMap, up to 1.2 km across.

  3. Make it yours

    Change the light from dawn to dusk, switch styles, take a guided tour, then save a 2400px picture or a GLB model.
